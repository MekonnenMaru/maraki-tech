Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026. This page explains what Maraki Tech collects when you visit maraki.tech and when you contact us.

Who we are

Maraki Tech builds software, payment technology, and digital products. Contact: hello@maraki.tech.

What we collect

  • Contact form: name, email, company, phone, country, city, service interest, and your message.
  • Campaign context: UTM parameters, Google click IDs when present, landing page, and referring site — so we know if a lead came from Google Ads or another channel.
  • Technical data: IP address and browser user agent with each message, used for spam control and rough location context.
  • Site usage: page visits counted for our dashboard. If you accept cookies, Google Analytics 4 and Google Ads tags may also measure traffic and conversions.

Why we use it

  • To reply to project inquiries and run our business.
  • To understand which campaigns and pages bring real leads.
  • To protect the site from spam and abuse.
  • To measure Google Ads performance when those tags are enabled.

Cookies & consent

Essential cookies keep the site working (for example a short visit marker and admin login). Analytics and advertising cookies load only after you choose Accept on the cookie banner. You can choose Essential only to decline measurement cookies.

Google may process measurement data under its own terms when tags are active. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

How long we keep data

Contact messages stay in our admin inbox until we delete them. Visit counts are stored as daily aggregates. Cookie consent is remembered in your browser until you clear site data.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may use Google Analytics / Google Ads as processors for measurement when you consent. We may disclose information if required by law.

Your choices

  • Decline analytics cookies via the banner.
  • Email us to ask about a message you sent or to request deletion where applicable.
  • Use browser controls to clear cookies and site storage.

Contact

Questions about privacy: hello@maraki.tech · Addis Ababa, Ethiopia.
